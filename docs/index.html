How these numbers work

Social time is the union of time spent online with at least one other player. Loner percentage is the remaining solo time divided by total play time. Shared sessions are contiguous overlaps between two players.

Each recorded death completes one observed lifetime. A lifetime begins at the previous death, or at the first tracked login, and adds only reconstructed online session time between those points; disconnected time is excluded.

Sessions without a clean logout are conservatively closed at a crash, restart, the end of known logs, or the configured maximum. They are counted as incomplete on player pages. Geyser logins come from successful Floodgate identity events, not connection probes.
